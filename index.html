FAQ

Questions, answered

Will I really save 50%?

That's what BurnRate is built for. The four kinds of waste it removes (oversized models, padded output, unused connectors and retry loops) typically make up a large share of AI coding spend. Your actual savings depend on how your team works, which is why BurnRate measures them on your real usage, and why the guarantee exists: if it doesn't save more than it costs in a month, that month is free.

Won't cheaper models and shorter answers hurt quality?

No, and that's the point of doing it carefully. Routing only sends a task to a cheaper model when it can handle it, and BurnRate tracks failure and retry rates per model, switching back automatically when quality drops. Concise profiles remove filler like intros, recaps and full-file reprints. They never limit the model's reasoning.

Doesn't each tool already show its own usage?

Some do, for that one tool only, and none of them will tell you a competitor would be cheaper. BurnRate combines every tool your team uses, finds the waste across all of them, and fixes it.

How does it get the data without seeing my code?

AI coding tools keep usage logs on your machine. Our open-source agent pulls out only the numbers (token counts, model and timestamp) and sends just those. Prompts, code and responses never leave your device.

Is it safe to install the connectors and skills you recommend?

Every recommendation comes with a trust check: who maintains it, how recently it was updated, how widely it's used and what permissions it asks for. We only link to original sources and never install anything for you.

Do I need to route traffic through a proxy?

No. Tracking, recommendations and concise profiles all work without one. The smart router is optional and runs locally on your machine.
